Deskvy is a lightweight Windows customization app: a text-only Home widget, a music visualizer above your taskbar, themes and accents, wallpapers — static or live — and favorite app tiles that snap into place.

Everything on your desktop, in one tray app

No Explorer hooks, no injection — Deskvy works through the safe layer of Windows customization, so it stays light and stays out of your way.

Home widget

Clock, date, city, and weather as plain text on your desktop — fully transparent, no card, no border. Drag it anywhere, lock it in place, pick the alignment, and set each line's size, font, and text shadow.

Theme & accent

Light and Dark themes, 8 accent presets, and toggles for accent on Start & taskbar and on title bars. Your accent applies system-wide through Windows itself.

Automatic theme schedule

Dark in the evening, Light in the morning — on by default at 18:00 / 06:00, with custom times in Pro. The schedule flips the theme on its own, even across midnight.

Music visualizer

A wave strip that lives above your taskbar and moves with whatever is playing. It appears when music is audible and hides when a fullscreen app takes over. White, Custom, and Aurora (song artwork) colors; 30 or 60 FPS.

Wallpaper, static or live

Browse a folder, preview, and apply static images in one click — or set a video as a live wallpaper that plays behind your icons and pauses for fullscreen apps. MP4 and WMV supported.

Favorite app tiles

Pin any app as a draggable tile anywhere on the desktop. Rename it, hide its name, toggle its shadow, and drag with Figma-style snap guides that line tiles up by edge and center. Lock everything from the tray.

The Windows warning is expected

Deskvy's installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen will warn you on the first install. That is normal for a small independent app. To install anyway:

  1. On the blue SmartScreen window, click More info.
  2. Click Run anyway.

Deskvy never asks for admin rights — it installs per-user, and its settings live in %LocalAppData%\Deskvy.

Privacy, plainly

Deskvy checks the weather online (Open-Meteo forecasts). Everything else — your widgets, favorites, wallpaper, and license — lives only on your machine. Your license key is validated offline and is never sent anywhere. There is no account, no tracking, and no ads.

FAQ

Does Deskvy work on Windows 10 and 11?

Yes, both. You need the .NET 10 Desktop Runtime (free from Microsoft) — the installer page links to it if you don't have it yet.

Does the live wallpaper cover my icons?

No. The video plays behind your desktop icons, and it pauses automatically when a true fullscreen app opens, resuming when you leave it.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. One lifetime key, no expiry, no hardware binding, no renewal. If you reinstall Windows or move to a new laptop, paste the same key again — you don't need a new one.

Will Deskvy slow down my PC?

Deskvy is built to stay light: no Explorer hooks, no injection, and the visualizer only renders while music is actually playing.
