Deskvy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Deskvy is a Windows desktop customization app made by an independent developer. There is no Deskvy account, no sign-in, and no Deskvy server. This policy describes exactly what the app sends, what it keeps on your machine, and what it never does.

Weather: the one thing that goes online

To show the weather on the Home widget, Deskvy refreshes roughly every 15 minutes:

  • It asks ip-api.com for an approximate location derived from your IP address (city and coordinates). Any request to that service necessarily reveals your IP address to it — that is how IP-based location works.
  • It then asks Open-Meteo for the current weather at those coordinates. No API key is used.

The location is used only to fetch the weather and display your city on the widget. It is not saved to Deskvy's settings file and is not sent to anyone else. If you never look at the weather, nothing else in Deskvy goes online at all.

What never leaves your machine

  • No identity: Deskvy never sends your name, email address, account details, or device identifiers anywhere. It has no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.
  • License keys stay offline: a Pro license key is validated on your own machine and is never transmitted to any server.
  • Your audio stays local: the music visualizer reads system audio on your machine only, to draw the wave. Audio is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.
  • Your files stay local: your wallpaper images and videos, favorite apps, and widget settings are read from your machine and never uploaded.

What is stored on your machine

Deskvy stores its settings, favorite app tiles, and license key locally in %LocalAppData%\Deskvy. That folder is separate from the install folder, so uninstalling Deskvy does not delete it — reinstalling keeps your setup. To remove it completely, delete that folder manually after uninstalling.

In short: the only data Deskvy ever sends out is an approximate location for weather (to ip-api.com and Open-Meteo). Everything else — widgets, favorites, wallpaper, license, audio — lives only on your PC.

Third-party services

ip-api.com and Open-Meteo have their own privacy policies, which govern what they do with the requests described above. Deskvy has no control over their practices and receives no data back from them beyond the location and weather itself.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of this page will change with it. Continued use of Deskvy after a change means you accept the updated policy.
